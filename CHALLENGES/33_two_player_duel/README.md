# TWO PLAYER DUEL

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Early  
**Recommended time:** 30 minutes  
**Players:** 2  
**Recommended prerequisites:** input, loops, conditions  

---

# RULES

- Two humans play on the same keyboard/terminal.
- Players take turns.
- Each has at least 2 possible actions.
- There must be a clear winner.

---

# SCORING

Score each category from **0–5** unless your group decides otherwise:

- Fairness
- Strategy
- Fun
- Polish
- Creativity

---

# WHAT THIS SECRETLY TEACHES

- turn systems
- fairness
