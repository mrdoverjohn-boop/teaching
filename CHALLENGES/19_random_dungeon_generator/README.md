# RANDOM DUNGEON GENERATOR

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Early  
**Recommended time:** 40 minutes  
**Players:** 1+  
**Recommended prerequisites:** lists, loops, random  

---

# RULES

- Generate a dungeon made of random rooms.
- Each room has at least a name and event.
- The dungeon must be different on repeated runs.
- At least 8 rooms are generated.

---

# SCORING

Score each category from **0–5** unless your group decides otherwise:

- Variety
- Randomness
- Presentation
- Creativity
- Replayability
