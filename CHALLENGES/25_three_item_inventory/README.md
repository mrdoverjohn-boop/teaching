# THREE ITEM INVENTORY

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Early  
**Recommended time:** 25 minutes  
**Players:** 1+  
**Recommended prerequisites:** lists, input, loops  

---

# RULES

- The player may carry only 3 items.
- At least 6 possible items exist.
- Choices force the player to decide what to keep.
- At least one ending depends on inventory.

---

# SCORING

Score each category from **0–5** unless your group decides otherwise:

- Decision Making
- Inventory Logic
- Creativity
- Fun
- Completeness

---

# WHAT THIS SECRETLY TEACHES

- inventory logic
- constraints

---

# OPTIONAL VARIANT

Items have weights instead of slot counts.
