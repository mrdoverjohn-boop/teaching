# SURVIVE 20 TURNS

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Early  
**Recommended time:** 35 minutes  
**Players:** 1+  
**Recommended prerequisites:** loops, random, variables  

---

# RULES

- The player must survive exactly 20 turns.
- Each turn contains a choice or random event.
- Track at least two resources like health, food, fuel, or money.
- Winning means reaching turn 20 alive.

---

# SCORING

Score each category from **0–5** unless your group decides otherwise:

- Balance
- Decisions
- Fun
- Completeness
- Replayability

# WHAT THIS SECRETLY TEACHES

- state management
- balancing
