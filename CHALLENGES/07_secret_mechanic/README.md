# SECRET MECHANIC

Everyone gets the same theme.

Everyone also secretly gets ONE required mechanic.

Nobody reveals theirs until the end.

---

# TIME

Recommended:

30–45 minutes.

---

# EXAMPLE THEME

DUNGEON

---

# SECRET MECHANICS

Write these onto shuffled cards or choose randomly:

- Trading
- Stealing
- Inventory
- Timer
- Random Events
- NPC
- Multiple Endings
- Boss Fight
- Healing
- Score System
- Limited Turns
- Secret Room
- Item Crafting
- Companion

---

# RULES

Your mechanic MUST meaningfully affect the game.

Bad example:

"I had to include an inventory so I printed the word INVENTORY once."

That does not count.

---

# REVEAL

At the end:

1. play everyone's game
2. guess what their secret mechanic was
3. reveal it

---

# SCORING

0–5 each:

- Fun
- Creativity
- Mechanic Integration
- Completeness
- Theme Use

---

# SECRET LESSON

Requirements are not always chosen by the programmer.

Sometimes somebody says:

"the game NEEDS trading"

and now it is your problem.

This challenge teaches adapting a design around a requirement.
